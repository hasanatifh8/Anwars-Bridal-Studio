# The Ultimate Guide to Choosing the Right Facial for Your Skin Type

Walking into a salon and picking a facial off the menu can feel overwhelming. The right choice really comes down to understanding your skin type first.

- **Oily skin**: look for clarifying or de-tan facials that control excess sebum
- **Dry skin**: hydrating facials with hyaluronic acid or aloe-based masks work best
- **Combination skin**: multi-masking facials that treat different zones differently
- **Sensitive skin**: fragrance-free, calming facials with minimal exfoliation

![Facial treatment](/images/gallery/skin-transformation.jpg)

A good esthetician will always ask about your skin history before recommending a treatment — if yours doesn't, that's a sign to ask more questions before you book.
