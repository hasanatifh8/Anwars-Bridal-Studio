# Nail Art Trends That Are Taking Over This Season

Nail art has moved on from loud, busy designs towards cleaner, more wearable looks. Here's what clients are requesting most at the salon right now.

- **Glazed donut nails** — a glossy, milky sheen that suits every outfit
- **Micro florals** — tiny hand-painted flowers on a neutral base
- **Chrome finish** — a metallic, mirror-like effect that catches the light
- **Negative space art** — minimal line work with visible bare nail

![Nail art trends](/images/world-class-speakers/speakers_4.png)

Gel polish remains the top choice for durability, especially chrome and glazed finishes which chip more easily with regular polish.
