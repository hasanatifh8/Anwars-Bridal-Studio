# Monsoon Hair Care: Protecting Your Hair from Frizz and Fall

Rain and humidity together create the perfect conditions for frizz, dandruff and hair fall. A few adjustments to your routine can prevent most of the seasonal damage.

- Dry hair completely before stepping out — damp hair breaks more easily
- Use an anti-frizz serum instead of heavier oils, which attract more humidity-driven frizz
- Wash hair more frequently if you're getting caught in the rain often, to avoid scalp buildup
- Switch to an anti-dandruff shampoo for the season, even as a precaution

![Monsoon hair care](/images/world-class-speakers/speakers_2.png)

If you're already noticing more hair fall than usual, don't wait it out — a scalp treatment can address it early before it becomes a longer-term issue.
