# Bridal Beauty Timeline: What to Do 6 Months Before Your Wedding

Brides often come to us a few weeks before the wedding wanting instant results. The truth is, the best bridal glow is built over months, not days.

**6 months before**: Start a monthly facial routine and address any specific skin concerns like pigmentation or acne scarring.

**3 months before**: Begin regular hair spa treatments if your hair needs strengthening, and finalise your bridal look with a trial session.

**1 month before**: Stick to your skincare routine, avoid trying new products, and schedule a final trial for hair and makeup.

**1 week before**: Manicure, pedicure, threading and a light hydrating facial — nothing aggressive this close to the big day.
