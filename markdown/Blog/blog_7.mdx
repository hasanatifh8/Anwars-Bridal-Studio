# Mehendi Design Trends for Every Celebration

Mehendi isn't just for brides anymore — every function in a wedding season, and plenty of celebrations beyond weddings, call for their own style of design. Here's what's trending right now.

- **Minimalist florals** — delicate, single-line floral patterns for daytime functions and guests
- **Full bridal coverage** — intricate patterns covering the hands and forearms, often personalised with the couple's initials or a small portrait
- **Arabic-style mehendi** — bold floral patterns with more open space, quicker to apply and just as striking
- **Two-tone effect** — combining regular and jagua-based paste for a contrast look on special request

![Mehendi design](/images/gallery/mehendi-01.jpg)

For brides, I always recommend a trial design a few weeks before the wedding — it lets us finalise the pattern and gives your hands time to recover before the real thing.
