# Why Regular Spa Visits Are Self-Care, Not a Luxury

Massages and spa treatments are often treated as an occasional treat rather than a routine part of wellness. But the benefits go beyond relaxation.

- Reduces muscle tension built up from desk work and poor posture
- Improves circulation, which supports skin and overall energy levels
- Lowers stress hormones, helping with sleep quality
- Gives you dedicated time to disconnect, which most routines don't allow for

![Spa and massage](/images/world-class-speakers/speakers_5.png)

You don't need a full spa day to feel the benefit — even a 30-minute head and shoulder massage once every few weeks makes a noticeable difference in how you carry stress physically.
