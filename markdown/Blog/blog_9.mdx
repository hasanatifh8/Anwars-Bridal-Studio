# Wedding Season Prep: A 3-Month Beauty Countdown

Wedding season usually means back-to-back functions, and it's easy to leave all the prep until the last week. A simple countdown makes it far more manageable.

**Month 3**: Start a monthly facial and hair spa routine, and get any hair colour touch-ups done early.

**Month 2**: Book trial sessions for makeup and hairstyling if you have a specific look in mind for the main functions.

**Month 1**: Focus on skin hydration and avoid trying new skincare products that could cause a reaction close to the events.

**Final week**: Manicure, pedicure, threading, and a light facial — keep it simple and avoid anything new or aggressive.
