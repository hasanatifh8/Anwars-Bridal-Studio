# Hair Colour & Botox: The Treatment Combo Our Founder Swears By

One of the most common concerns clients bring to me is hair that looks fine in colour but feels rough, dry or frizzy to the touch. Colour alone doesn't fix texture — that's where a hair botox treatment comes in.

- **Colour** refreshes shade, covers greys and adds dimension to hair
- **Hair botox** is a deep-conditioning treatment that smooths the hair shaft, reduces frizz and adds shine without the harsh chemicals of a keratin treatment
- Doing them together in one sitting saves multiple salon visits and gives more even, lasting results
- Results typically last 2-3 months with the right at-home care in between

![Hair colour and botox treatment](/images/world-class-speakers/speakers_1.png)

I always assess hair texture and damage level first — botox works beautifully on frizzy, dry hair, but isn't necessary for everyone. A quick consultation tells us exactly what your hair needs.
