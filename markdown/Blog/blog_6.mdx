# The Modern Man's Guide to Grooming: Beard, Skin and Hair

More men are investing time in grooming than ever before, but a lot of them are still working with outdated routines. Here's what actually makes a difference.

- Trim your beard every 2-3 weeks to keep the shape sharp, even if you're growing it out
- Use a light moisturiser after shaving — skipping this is the most common mistake
- Get a haircut every 3-4 weeks to avoid the "grown out" look between visits
- A monthly head massage isn't just relaxing, it also supports scalp health

![Men's grooming](/images/world-class-speakers/speakers_1.png)

A hot towel shave once a month at the salon also does wonders — it exfoliates the skin underneath the beard, something a home razor simply can't replicate.
