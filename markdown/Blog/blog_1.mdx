# 5 Everyday Hair Care Mistakes You Should Stop Making

Most hair damage doesn't come from a single bad decision — it builds up from small habits repeated every day. As a stylist, these are the mistakes I see most often in the chair.

- Washing hair with water that's too hot, which strips natural oils
- Brushing wet hair from the roots instead of the ends
- Skipping heat-protectant spray before styling
- Tying hair too tightly in the same spot every day
- Going too long between trims, letting split ends travel upward

![Hair care routine](/images/blog/blog_1.png)

The good news is that none of these require expensive products to fix — just a change in routine. Switching to lukewarm water, using a wide-tooth comb on wet hair, and trimming every 8-10 weeks makes a visible difference within a couple of months.

---

If your hair already feels dry, brittle or prone to breakage, a hair spa treatment can help restore moisture and shine before you rebuild these habits at home.
