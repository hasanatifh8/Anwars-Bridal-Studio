# 2026's Biggest Hair Colour Trends You Need to Try

Colour trends move fast, but a few clear favourites have emerged this year — and they all share one thing in common: low maintenance without sacrificing impact.

- **Butter blonde** — a soft, warm blonde that suits most skin tones
- **Espresso brunette** — a glossy, deep brown with cool undertones
- **Caramel balayage** — hand-painted highlights that grow out naturally
- **Copper red** — a bold statement colour making a strong comeback

![Hair colour trends](/images/gallery/bridal-03.jpg)

The key to any of these looks lasting well is a proper strand test and using colour-safe products at home. Sulphate-free shampoo alone can extend colour vibrancy by weeks.
